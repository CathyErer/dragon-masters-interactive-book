# Original demonstration assets

The current demonstration art is **code-generated SVG**, not an AI-painted 2.5D book. `scripts/build_demo.py` contains its complete reproducible drawing source: a fictional lighthouse workshop, Mina, an empty mechanism and a separate brass gear. The lit state changes the lamp and its light, with the same geometry. There are no third-party character pictures, scans or private classroom images in this package.

`scripts/generate_score.py` produces three deterministic original instrumental WAV sketches. It uses synthesized tones, not sampled songs, and requires Python's standard library only. These sketches demonstrate mood switching, not professional orchestral quality.

The complete higher-quality image/editing and music prompts are in the Skill references. New book artwork and its source/reference permissions must be recorded in the user's own project. The included vector demo does not claim to reproduce a third-party book or the visual finish of a separately illustrated adaptation.
